The person behind the products

A bit about how I got here

I studied Computer Engineering at the University of Waterloo (2016–2021), where co-op took me through mobile engineering at a digital-health startup, backend work at Fleet Complete, and a research role applying spectral imaging to water-quality analysis.

After graduating I co-founded SeedCI (YC candidate), building unsupervised-learning cyberattack detection, before moving into full-stack consulting at Avanade, shipping EV-charging features for Ford's FordPass app and automating cloud infrastructure that cut a client's costs by $1M a year.

Today I split my time between security, fraud, and identity & access management (IAM) engineering at EQ Bank, where I partner with the IAM team on a live SSO/Okta identity migration during the PC Financial and EQ Bank merger, help build the unified app, and ship WebAuthn passkey (passwordless) support on iOS. I also run LEYOXA, the operating layer connecting AI across a dental practice, alongside Praxis, a Realtime-API + ElevenLabs voice AI agent integrated into NexHealth's EHR, live with paying dental clinics across North America.
