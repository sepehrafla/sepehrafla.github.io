01Customer IAM for a bank merger

The challenge. Move EQ Bank’s customer identity platform from HID to Okta and roll out passwordless authentication, while EQ Bank and PC Financial converge into one app, with zero lockouts.

On the CIAM team I built FIDO2 passkey authentication on iOS (WebAuthn through AuthenticationServices, bound to the app with Associated Domains), and OAuth 2.0 session management on the Okta iOS SDK: tokens held in the Keychain, refreshed silently, and sender‑constrained with DPoP so a stolen token can’t be replayed. Then I traced the sign‑in and launch critical path with Instruments and removed the slow calls.

90%of 500K customers on passkeys
3×faster sign‑in, 15s to 5s
<1sapp launch, down from 6s
4.5Mcustomers on the converged app

The result. Phishing‑resistant, faster authentication at bank scale, and the bank decommissioned third‑party services it no longer needed.

CIAM · Okta · OAuth 2.0 · DPoP · FIDO2 / WebAuthn · Passkeys · Keychain · Swift · Instruments

02Production AI agents at Praxis

The challenge. Put LLM voice agents on live phone lines for dental practices: hold a real‑time conversation, read and write appointments in each practice’s own practice‑management system, and stay inside HIPAA, with protected health information under a business associate agreement.

For a dental marketing company I architected and shipped Praxis: real‑time voice agents with LLM tool calling over telephony, a scheduling layer that validates every slot before it books, read/write integrations with the practices’ systems, and agents for reputation, search visibility, call coaching and owner reporting. Every call is evaluated on what the agent actually did, not on a demo.

The result. Lower operating costs and more clients for the company. It started as a project and became a partnership.

03Connected‑vehicle charging at Ford

The challenge. Shift when EV owners charge to off‑peak hours without making them think about it, and prove whether they actually adopt it.

At Avanade I built the iOS side of scheduled and manual charging in the FordPass app, against Ford’s connected‑vehicle services, and the analytics behind it: how many vehicles were enrolled and how many owners chose automatic over manual charging.

The result. Owners who opted in charged off‑peak, and Ford had measured adoption across its EV owners.

Swift · Connected vehicle · Charge scheduling · Product analytics
