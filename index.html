01Making bank sign‑in fast and phishing‑proof

The challenge. Change how customers sign in to their money while two banks, EQ Bank and PC Financial, merge into one app, without locking anyone out.

I built passkey sign‑in with Face ID, so there’s no password to steal. I profiled every call on the sign‑in and launch path and fixed the slow ones, and worked on OAuth 2.0 session management with tokens bound to the device.

90%of 500K customers moved to passkeys
3×faster sign‑in, 15s to 5s

4.5Mcustomers on the converged app

The result. Safer, faster sign‑in for millions of customers, and the bank dropped third‑party services it no longer needed.

02Builder of the AI platform behind Praxis

The challenge. Put AI on the phone for real dental practices: answer patients live, book straight into decades‑old practice software, and handle patient data under HIPAA rules.

For a dental marketing company, I built Praxis, shipping one agent after another until six were running in production: voice booking, reputation, search visibility, an assistant for owners and more.

03EV charging for Ford

The challenge. Get drivers to charge when electricity is cheap, without making them think about it, and prove whether they actually do.

At Avanade, I built automatic and manual charging schedules into the FordPass iPhone app, and the measurement behind them: how many owners enrolled, and how many chose automatic over manual charging.

The result. Owners who opted in charged off‑peak, and Ford could see real adoption across its EV owners.
