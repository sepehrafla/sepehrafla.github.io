01 · About

A bit about how I got here

I studied Computer Engineering at the University of Waterloo (2016–2021), where co-op took me through mobile engineering at a digital-health startup, backend work at Fleet Complete, and a research role applying spectral imaging to water-quality analysis.

After graduating I co-founded SeedCI (YC candidate), building unsupervised-learning cyberattack detection and a 97%-accurate real-estate price estimator, before moving into full-stack consulting at Avanade — shipping EV-charging features for Ford's FordPass app and automating cloud infrastructure that cut a client's costs by $1M a year.

Today I split my time between security & fraud engineering at EQ Bank — migrating auth infrastructure to Okta and shipping passkey support on iOS — and running LEYOXA, where I build AI-ready platforms and voice agents for dental clinics across North America.
